My research centers the perspectives of people of color (PoC) and investigates how they navigate changing hierarchies, ultimately interested in how these processes shape intergroup relations, political attitudes, and hierarchical views.

Specifically, I investigate 1) contexts and consequences of people’s perceived group position, 2) the interplay between identity and politics, and 3) the contents of identities.

While I'm not doing research, you'll see me trying to master many different crafts: ice skating, rock climbing, pottery, painting, and dancing.
